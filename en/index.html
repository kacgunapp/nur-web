Nur: Prayer Times & Qur'an

A simple companion that makes it easier to keep your worship regular. It has kids and adult modes; it asks for no account, shows no ads and collects no data.

For iOS · coming soon to the App Store

What's inside?

🕰️

Prayer times

12 calculation methods including Diyanet, high latitude rules, prayer time notifications and a monthly timetable.

🧎

3D prayer and wudu

Step-by-step movements with a man, woman or child figure; rotate to any angle, with the supplications alongside.

📖

Qur'an

114 surahs, page-by-page reading, transliteration, verse-by-verse listening from five reciters, bookmarks and a khatm plan.

🕋

Qibla

Compass and map together: the direction of the Kaaba, the straight-line distance and nearby mosques.

📿

Dhikr counter

A counter with prayer beads; different bead materials and a light vibration on each tap.

🧰

Tools

Zakat and fitra calculation, Hijri calendar and blessed days, suhoor, tahajjud and Jumu'ah reminders.

📱

Widget

The next prayer and today's tasks on your home screen; tap it to open the right section.

Your privacy stays with you

  • No account, sign-in or server.
  • No ads, analytics or tracking.
  • Your progress and settings stay only on your device.
  • Your location is used on the device for prayer times and the Qibla; for the nearby mosque search only a version rounded to about 1 km is sent.
