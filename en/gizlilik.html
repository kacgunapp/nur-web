Privacy Policy

Effective date: 27 September 2026

This policy explains what information the Nur: Prayer Times & Qur'an app for iOS (“Nur”) uses and how. In short: Nur has no server and collects no personal data from you. Some features send requests to the third-party services listed one by one below in order to work.

No account

To use Nur you don't need to create an account, sign in or provide details such as an email address or phone number. The app has no server.

No ads, analytics or tracking

Nur shows no ads; it contains no analytics, crash reporting or tracking tools. It doesn't track you across apps and websites and doesn't access the advertising identifier.

Your data stays on your device

The name you optionally enter, the city you choose, your settings, your prayer and task progress, your Qur'an reading progress and your bookmarks are stored only on your device. If it's on, this data is also included in your iPhone's iCloud backup; that backup belongs to your Apple account and cannot be read by Nur. Verse audio you download also stays on your device, but because it can be downloaded again it isn't backed up; iOS may delete it to free up space, and it's downloaded again when needed. If you turn on the home screen widget, a short summary (the next prayer, today's tasks) is written to the shared area on your device so the widget can show it.

The profile you choose on first launch (child, adult man or adult woman; for a child, girl or boy) and your school are also stored only on your device; they're used for the prayer narration, the figure and the Asr time calculation, and are never sent anywhere.

When you delete the app, all Nur data on the device is deleted with it.

Special days mode

Special days mode, which can be turned on in the woman and girl profiles, and the record of which days it was on are kept only in the app's own storage, on your device. This information is never sent to any server; it isn't written to the shared area the home screen widget reads, doesn't appear in notification text and isn't recorded (no logs are kept). The widget and notifications are the same whether special days mode is on or off. When you turn it off, past days stay on the device for at most 90 days, only for the streak calculation; when you delete the app this information is deleted too. If it's on, it's included in your iPhone's iCloud backup together with your other app data.

Location

If you grant location permission, your location is used on your device to calculate prayer times and the Qibla direction. If you don't grant location permission, you can pick your city from the list.

When you open the “Nearby mosques” screen, your location is rounded to about 1 km (two decimal places) and sent to the OpenStreetMap Overpass service to find mosques nearby. As with every internet request, your IP address also reaches this service; no other information that identifies you is sent. The city found from your location is stored on the device at the same precision, rounded to about 1 km. Mosque data © OpenStreetMap contributors (ODbL).

Map and directions (Apple Maps)

The maps on the “Nearby mosques” and “Kaaba” screens are shown with Apple Maps. Map imagery is loaded from Apple for the region shown on screen; if you have granted location permission, your location is also shown on the map. This service is subject to Apple's privacy policy.

When you tap a mosque's “Directions” button, Apple Maps opens with the mosque's name and coordinates; the route is calculated in Apple Maps. This happens only when you tap.

Qur'an audio

Verse audio is played from, or downloaded to your device from, the Islamic Network service (cdn.islamic.network). With each request, this service receives: your IP address, the address of the requested file (which reciter's surah and verse is being listened to) and the standard browser identifier (User-Agent) containing the app and iOS versions. No other information is sent. Audio downloaded to the device plays without a network. The recordings are the copyright of the reciters; the source and terms of use are in the app under About › Sources and licences.

Sharing

When you tap the “Share my progress” button, your progress summary (streak, goal, prayer and dhikr counts) opens in the iOS share sheet. The text goes only to the app or person you choose; Nur never shares anything on its own.

Notifications

Prayer time notifications and reminders are scheduled locally on your device. Remote (push) notifications are not used; no registration with any server is made for notifications.

Children

Nur has a kids mode. Because the app collects no personal data from any user, including children, there is no data processing specific to children either.

Changes

If this policy changes, the current version will be published on this page with a new effective date.
