Support

Common situations and their fixes are below. If you can't find your answer, write to us.

Frequently asked questions

Prayer times look wrong

Times are calculated from the selected location and calculation method. Check these in order:

  1. Location: Me tab › Location. Tap “Find my location automatically” or pick your city from the list. If another city is selected, the times will be for that city.
  2. Calculation method: Me tab › Calculation method. For Turkey choose Presidency of Religious Affairs (Diyanet); “Automatic by city” picks the method of the country you're in.
  3. Asr time: On the same screen, check the Earlier Asr (Diyanet) or Later Asr choice; the two can differ by close to an hour.
  4. Hijri date: If the Hijri calendar is off by a day, you can set a ±2 day correction in Tools › Hijri calendar.

A difference of a few minutes from the official calendar can come from a safety margin and is normal.

Notifications aren't arriving
  1. On your iPhone open Settings › Notifications › Nur; “Allow Notifications” must be on.
  2. If a Focus mode (Do Not Disturb, Sleep) is on, add Nur to the allowed apps.
  3. In the app, Me tab › Adhan notification must be on. For suhoor, tahajjud and Jumu'ah reminders, see Tools › Reminders.
  4. When you change your location, notifications are rescheduled automatically for the new times.
  5. iOS limits how many notifications an app can schedule in advance; Nur plans about a week of prayer times. If you open the app at least once a week, notifications continue without interruption.
The Qibla direction drifts
  1. Calibrate the compass by moving the phone through the air in a figure 8 a few times.
  2. Move away from magnetic cases, speakers, metal tables or electronic devices; they throw the compass off.
  3. Make sure Settings › Privacy & Security › Location Services › System Services › Compass Calibration is on.
  4. Make sure your location is correct: the Qibla angle is calculated from the selected location.
Qur'an audio won't play or download
  1. Verse audio comes from the internet. Check your Wi-Fi or cellular connection; in Settings › Cellular, Nur's mobile data permission must be on.
  2. If a download stopped halfway, try again: partially downloaded files are deleted and the download completes from the start.
  3. Downloaded surahs play without a network. You can see and delete downloads under Read the Qur'an › Reading settings.
  4. The listen button is only available for supplications taken from Qur'an verses; other supplications have no button.
